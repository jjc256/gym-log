All-time records

Each day's lowest-RIR working set supplies the estimate. Dashed lines track the heaviest one-rep set recorded by that date. Exercise, location, equipment, load view, unit, and dates apply; rep, effort, and warmup filters only affect the set view below.

How the estimate works

Load × (1 + (reps + RIR) ÷ 30), using Epley with RIR added to completed reps. One rep at zero RIR uses the lifted load. Ties for lowest RIR use the higher estimate. RPE-only sets and sets without RIR do not produce estimates. More than 10 effective reps are marked as less certain.
