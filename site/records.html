How these records are calculated

Each day's lowest-RIR working set supplies the implied 1RM. Ties use the higher estimate. Highest implied 1RM is the best of those daily estimates. Actual 1RM is the heaviest logged one-rep set, regardless of RIR.

Epley estimate: load × (1 + (reps + RIR) ÷ 30). One rep at zero RIR uses the lifted load. Missing RIR and RPE-only sets do not produce estimates. Estimates from more than 10 effective reps (reps + RIR) are marked as less certain. Machine figures use the recorded labels.

Machine labels are only comparable on consistent equipment.
